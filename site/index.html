POPIA SecureCheck

A plain-language check of how your business looks after personal information. The questions follow the eight conditions for lawful processing in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013, with extra weight on security safeguards (section 19) and breach notification (section 22).

Not legal advice and not a POPIA compliance certification. SecureCheck helps a small business see gaps in its own answers. It does not measure every duty in the Act, it does not know facts you do not tell it, and a high score does not mean the Information Regulator would agree. For official guidance, use the Information Regulator or a qualified professional. The Act is the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

How to use it

  1. Answer yes, partly, or no. Partly means you have started, but the practice is incomplete or not consistent. Partly scores half the question's weight.
  2. The score is calculated in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Leave "Remember my answers on this device" ticked only if you want them kept in local storage.
  3. Download a Markdown file or print the results, then work through the fixes. Clear the saved answers when you are done on a shared computer.
